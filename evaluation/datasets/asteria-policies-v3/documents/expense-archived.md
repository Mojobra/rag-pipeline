# Expense Reimbursement Policy (Archived)

Policy ID: EXP-042
Status: Archived on 2025-12-31. Superseded by EXP-204.

Under this historical policy, employees needed an itemized receipt only for
purchases above EUR 50. That threshold does not govern claims made under the
current policy. This document is retained for historical questions, not as
current reimbursement guidance.
