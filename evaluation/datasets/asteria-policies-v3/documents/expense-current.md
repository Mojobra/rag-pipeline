# Expense Reimbursement Policy (Current)

Policy ID: EXP-204
Status: Current since 2026-01-01. This policy supersedes EXP-042.

Employees must attach an itemized receipt for each purchase above EUR 25.
For a purchase of exactly EUR 25, an itemized receipt is not mandatory under
this threshold. A payment-card slip without line-item details does not count
as an itemized receipt. If a merchant cannot provide a receipt, the employee
must submit a missing-receipt declaration with the date, amount, supplier, and
business purpose.

Claims of EUR 500 or less require manager approval. Claims above EUR 500
require department-director approval before submission. Employees must submit
claims within 30 calendar days of the purchase date. These approval boundaries
are not a maximum reimbursable claim amount.
