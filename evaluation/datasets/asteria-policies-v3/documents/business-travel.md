# Business Travel Policy

Policy ID: TRV-108
Status: Current.

Employees must book flights through the Asteria Travel Portal. Economy class
is required for flight segments shorter than six hours. Premium economy is
permitted for segments of six hours or longer only when a manager approves it
before booking. Flight duration means one scheduled segment, not the total
itinerary.

Travel meals follow the current Expense Reimbursement Policy, EXP-204, and
require its supporting evidence. Travel Operations handles booking questions;
Finance reviews reimbursement claims after travel.
