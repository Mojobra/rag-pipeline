# Remote Work Policy

Policy ID: REM-075

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
The manager may require office attendance for customer meetings, secure
workflows, training, or team events. Remote work is not an entitlement and does
not change the employee's normal working hours or performance expectations.

## Work Location

The approved remote location must be in the employee's country of employment
unless People Operations and Tax approve a temporary exception in writing.
Employees must keep their work-location record current. A permanent move
requires a separate employment and tax review.

## Security and Equipment

When using public Wi-Fi, employees must connect through the company VPN before
accessing company systems. Confidential calls must not be held where
unauthorized people can hear them. Company devices may not be shared with
family members or other third parties.

Asteria provides one standard monitor, keyboard, and mouse for an approved home
workspace. Internet service and optional furniture remain the employee's
responsibility unless local law requires otherwise.
