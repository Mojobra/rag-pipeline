# Information Security Incident Policy

Policy ID: SEC-210

Employees share responsibility for protecting company systems and information.
They must not investigate suspected attacks on their own, contact a suspected
attacker, or delete evidence. Security Operations coordinates containment and
decides whether Legal, Privacy, or external authorities must be involved.

## Suspicious Messages

Employees must report suspected phishing to Security Operations through the
security portal within 30 minutes of discovery. The employee should preserve
the message and use the portal's phishing-report function. The message must not
be forwarded to colleagues because forwarding can spread unsafe links or
attachments.

## Lost Devices and Credentials

A lost or stolen company device must be reported to Security Operations and the
employee's manager within one hour. Security Operations may remotely lock or
erase the device. If credentials may have been exposed, the employee must
change the affected password from a trusted device and follow any additional
containment instructions.

For an immediate threat to personal safety, employees should contact local
emergency services before completing the internal report.
