# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Policy ID: EXP-042

This policy applies to employees seeking reimbursement for reasonable business
expenses paid with personal funds. Expenses must have a clear business purpose,
use the lowest practical cost, and comply with any department budget limits.
Personal purchases, traffic fines, minibar charges, and optional travel
insurance are not reimbursable.

## Required Evidence

Employees must attach an itemized receipt for every purchase above EUR 25. A
payment-card slip without line-item details is not an itemized receipt. When a
merchant cannot provide a receipt, the employee must submit a missing-receipt
declaration explaining the date, amount, supplier, and business purpose.

## Approval and Submission

Claims up to and including EUR 500 require the employee's manager to approve
them. Claims above EUR 500 require department-director approval before
submission. Employees must submit claims within 30 calendar days of the
purchase date. Finance may return an incomplete claim without payment and ask
the employee to correct the evidence.

Approved reimbursements are paid in the next available payroll cycle. Questions
about tax treatment are handled by Finance, not by the employee's manager.
