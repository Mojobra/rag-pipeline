# Records Retention Policy

Policy ID: RET-330

Business records must be retained for the period assigned to their record
category and then deleted through an approved process. Employees must store
records in company systems rather than personal accounts or local archives.
The record owner is responsible for applying the correct category.

## Retention Periods

Closed customer support tickets are retained for 24 months after closure.
Expense records are retained for seven years after the end of the fiscal year
in which the expense was recorded. Routine security-access logs are retained
for 12 months unless they are associated with an investigation.

## Holds and Deletion

A legal hold overrides the standard deletion schedule until Legal releases the
hold in writing. Employees must not alter or delete records covered by a hold,
even when the normal retention period has expired. Legal notifies affected
record owners when a hold begins and ends.

When no hold applies, system owners must use approved deletion jobs and retain
evidence that the deletion completed. Moving a record to another folder does
not satisfy the deletion requirement.
