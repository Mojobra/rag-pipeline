# Business Travel Policy

Policy ID: TRV-108

Employees must book flights and hotels through the Asteria Travel Portal.
Bookings made elsewhere require a documented portal outage or written approval
from Travel Operations. Travelers should choose practical routes and avoid
optional fees that do not support the business purpose of the trip.

## Air Travel

Economy class is required for flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy
is permitted for flights of six hours or longer when a manager approves it
before booking. Business class requires vice-president approval and a medical
or operational justification. Flight duration means the scheduled time of one
flight segment, not the total itinerary.

## Lodging and Changes

The standard hotel limit is EUR 180 per night before local taxes. A traveler may
exceed the limit when the conference hotel is required or Travel Operations
documents that no compliant room is reasonably available. Travelers must
cancel unused reservations promptly. The employee is responsible for avoidable
no-show charges.

Travel meals follow the Expense Reimbursement Policy and require the same
supporting evidence. Travel Operations handles booking questions, while Finance
reviews reimbursement claims after travel.
