# Records Retention Schedule

## Evidence requirements

Every retention request must identify the owner, the business purpose, the
record category, the date on which the record was created, and the legal hold
status. The owner must confirm whether the record contains personal data,
financial data, or customer correspondence before the request can be approved.
For a mixed record, the longest applicable retention period governs until the
records team has reviewed the exceptions. A request without an owner cannot
be processed, even when the requested period appears in the schedule.

## Review and release

The records team checks active legal holds before approving deletion. A hold
overrides the ordinary disposal date, and the owner must not remove the record
while the hold remains active. When a hold ends, the team recalculates the
disposal date from the original creation date and the applicable category,
records the decision, and sends a confirmation to the owner. Approval is not
evidence that deletion occurred; a separate completion record is required.

## Exceptions

If a system cannot delete one item without affecting other retained material,
the owner must request a documented exception. The exception must identify the
system, affected categories, compensating access controls, planned remediation
date, and reviewer. The records team may reject an exception that lacks a
credible remediation plan. Approved exceptions are reviewed every quarter and
expire when the limitation is removed. The owner remains responsible for
preventing unauthorized access during the exception period.
